Experience

My related experience in GIS, conservation, and applied spatial research.

Virginia Department of Conservation and Recreation ('25 - present)

Conservation Data Specialist

I support Virginia DCR's dam safety and flood risk programs by building and maintaining the GIS infrastructure behind them, including the Virginia Flood Risk Information System (VFRIS) and Dam Safety Inventory System (DSIS). These sites are critical public services and allow folks to access important information about local risks and resources. I do both routine data upkeep and more involved spatial analysis and application development for agency planning and reporting.

NOAA NCCOS, Coastal Resilience Branch ('23 - '25)

Product Development Specialist

I translated our agency's work into public-facing products, including story maps, dashboards, and infographics. The goal of these products was to help coastal managers and stakeholders make decisions related to their coastal resilience.

St. Croix, USVI & Monterey, California ('20 - '24)

Graduate Researcher — Spatial Ecology of Leatherback Sea Turtles

My thesis research centered on tracking leatherback sea turtles to better understand how they use habitat during the breeding season. Working with federal and nonprofit partners, I helped deploy satellite tags on nesting females on St. Croix and followed their movements during and beyond the nesting season. I presented this work at numerous conferences, including the International Sea Turtle Symposium (ISTS 2021, ISTS 2022) and Western Society of Naturalists (WSN 2022).

Upwell Turtles, Monterey, California ('21 - '22)

Observer

I was part of a crew that flew aerial line-transect surveys in a small, low-flying plane off the coast of Monterey, spotting and logging sea turtles, marine mammals, and jellyfish. The data fed into broader assessments of species distribution and density for NOAA and academic collaborators.

University Corporation, Monterey, California ('21 - '22)

GIS Analyst

I applied geostatistical methods to build raster surfaces mapping cell coverage by carrier for California utilities researchers. Beyond the analysis itself, I put together training materials and QA/QC documentation and walked the client through them directly, and I built a custom geoprocessing toolbox to speed up the workflow going forward.

Timmons Group, Richmond, Virginia ('19 - '21)

GIS Specialist / GIS Technician

I worked on a variety of GIS projects in this role. I conducted GIS analysis for clients in projects spanning environmental resource management, utilities mapping, tax parcel updates, and local transportation. I worked both independently and on teams and often trained other colleagues on workflows.

University of Richmond, School of Professional and Continuing Studies ('19 - '21)

Teaching Assistant

I supported instruction across GIS Fundamentals, Advanced GIS, and Field Applications courses, running hands-on demos, building lesson content, answering student questions during office hours and labs, and helping to guide independent research projects.

St. Croix Sea Turtle Project / USFWS / NOAA, Sandy Point National Wildlife Refuge ('17, '18, '19)

Leatherback Sea Turtle Research Assistant

I'm very passionate about sea turtle research and conservation! In this position, over many leatherback nesting seasons, I led and participated in data collection through rigorous all-night fieldwork, including sea turtle tagging and genetic sampling, and communicated project goals and results through presentations and written media. I collected, maintained, manipulated, and analyzed project geospatial data using Microsoft Access/Excel and ArcGIS, and completed data entry and QA/QC of historic and current spatial and tabular data to ensure it met project standards.

Department of Geography and the Environment, University of Richmond ('18 - '19)

Research Assistant & Part-Time GIS Technician

In this role, I had various responsibilities spanning GIS analysis, mentorship of eight college and high school interns, faculty research support, and student assistance. A large part of my analytical work was in support of the Kenya Settlement Scheme Project, an international partnership that required strong communication, collaboration, documentation, and technical skills. I also contributed to work in Richmond census, campus trees, water quality, and national park mapping.

Coral Restoration Foundation, Key Largo, Florida ('18)

Coral Conservation and Reef Restoration Intern

I conducted in-water, intensive fieldwork restoring endangered staghorn and elkhorn coral populations, including coral outplanting, offshore nursery maintenance, boating and trailering, and scientific monitoring of restoration success. Alongside the fieldwork, I analyzed restoration outcomes using GIS and built web applications to help the organization communicate its impact.

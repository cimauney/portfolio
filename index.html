About

I'm a GIS professional working at the intersection of geospatial technology and environmental conservation, with experience ranging from flood risk and dam safety mapping in Virginia to coastal resilience tools with NOAA and leatherback sea turtle research across the Caribbean and Pacific. Along the way, I've built a broad skill set in spatial analysis, Python scripting, web application development, and data visualization — all in service of making complex spatial data clear and useful to the people who rely on it, from state agencies to research teams to coastal communities. This portfolio highlights some of the projects and roles that have shaped that path, so take a look around and feel free to reach out if you'd like to talk more.

Experience

Conservation Data Specialist

Virginia Department of Conservation and Recreation — Jan 2025–present

In this role, I support Virginia's dam safety and flood risk programs by building and maintaining the GIS infrastructure behind them, including the Virginia Flood Risk Information System (VFRIS) and Dam Safety Inventory System (DSIS). Day to day, that means a mix of routine data upkeep and more involved spatial analysis and application development for agency planning and reporting. I also spend a fair amount of time on the documentation side — writing metadata, user guides, and workflow notes so that the tools and data I build are actually usable by the colleagues who inherit them.

Graduate Researcher — Spatial Ecology of Leatherback Sea Turtles

St. Croix, USVI & Monterey, California — Aug 2020–2024

My thesis research centered on tracking leatherback sea turtles to better understand how they use habitat during the breeding season. Working with federal and nonprofit partners, I helped deploy satellite tags on nesting females on St. Croix and followed their movements well beyond the nesting season. Analyzing that tracking data in R and ArcGIS Pro let me map out key in-water habitat areas, classify different behavioral states, and tie movement patterns to specific environmental variables.

Observer

GIS Analyst

University Corporation, Monterey, California — Aug 2021–Jul 2022

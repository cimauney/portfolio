Marsh Impacts of Sea-Level Rise

NOAA NCCOS · ArcGIS Experience Builder, ArcGIS Pro · 2024

An interactive ArcGIS Experience exploring how marsh vegetation composition shifts under different sea-level rise scenarios along the northern Gulf of Mexico. The app lets users step through projected high, mid, and low marsh distribution from 2020 to 2100 under three SLR scenarios, alongside land cover acreage trends and custom reports summarizing projected marsh loss.

Dam Safety Risk Viewer

VA Dept. of Conservation and Recreation · ArcGIS Enterprise, Python · 2025

A statewide web application built to help dam owners and regional staff quickly assess inundation risk and inspection status across Virginia's regulated dams. The tool pulls from the Dam Safety Inventory System (DSIS) and layers in downstream hazard classifications, automating what was previously a manual cross-reference between spreadsheets and paper files.

Leatherback Internesting Habitat Model

Moss Landing Marine Laboratories · R, ArcGIS Pro · 2023

A spatial analysis of satellite tag data from nesting leatherback sea turtles on St. Croix, used to delineate core internesting habitat and characterize the environmental conditions associated with it. The resulting habitat maps were shared with refuge managers to help inform vessel-traffic guidance during the nesting season.
